# Community guidelines

FrostByte Feedback is a place to improve the product, share clear reports, and help other users. Everyone should be able to participate respectfully, regardless of background, identity, experience, or ability.

## How to participate

- Be specific about the product behavior or idea you want to discuss.
- Give people room to ask questions and learn. Assume good intent while addressing harmful behavior when it occurs.
- Critique ideas and implementations without personal attacks.
- Respect people's privacy. Get permission before sharing someone else's information or private correspondence.
- Keep reports relevant to FrostByte and share only material you have permission to use.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized content, doxxing, impersonation, spam, and repeated disruption are not acceptable. This applies to issues, pull requests, comments, and other spaces managed for this repository.

## Reporting a concern

Report conduct concerns privately to [support@blackfrostai.com](mailto:support@blackfrostai.com). Include a link to the relevant public content and a short explanation. Share only the personal information needed to understand the concern. For technical vulnerabilities, follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

## Moderation

Maintainers may request edits, remove content, lock a discussion, close a thread, or restrict participation according to the situation. An initial reminder may be enough for a minor problem; serious or repeated harm may require immediate restrictions. When practical, maintainers will explain the reason and a way to clarify or appeal through the support address.

Moderation aims to keep the space useful and welcoming while allowing honest criticism and constructive disagreement. Reports will be handled with discretion, but do not include information that is unnecessary for the concern.
